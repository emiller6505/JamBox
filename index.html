JamBox

A minimalist macOS music player. Gapless by design.

Download for macOS Free · macOS 14+

Built for people who care about music.

  • Gapless playback

    The whole point. Tracks flow into each other with no audible seam.

  • Local files only

    No accounts, no streaming, no library import. Pick a folder, get a track list.

  • Wide format support

    MP3, M4A, FLAC, AIFF, WAV, ALAC, AAC. FLAC durations done right.

  • Album art that just works

    Pulled from embedded metadata or a cover image sitting next to your tracks.

  • Three themes

    Light, Dark, and Candy. Pick your mood; it sticks across launches.

  • Instant search

    Filter by title, artist, or album as you type. No indexing wait.
